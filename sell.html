Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, straightforward answers to essential questions regarding the luxury selling process.

How does VP Signature Group determine my property's listing price?

+

We conduct an exhaustive Comparative Market Assessment (CMA) analyzing hyper-local closed transactions, pending contracts, active competition, and seasonal buyer momentum. We factor in unique architectural improvements, premium lot characteristics, and current interest rates to establish a price that maximizes return without languishing on the market.

How do you reach high-net-worth and out-of-state buyers?

+

Over 60% of Central Florida luxury buyers relocate from the Northeast, California, the Midwest, and international hubs. We deploy hyper-targeted digital advertising campaigns, syndication on premier international luxury portals, and direct outreach through our private network of wealth advisors and executive relocation directors.

Can I sell my property privately without public open houses?

+

Yes. Discretion is paramount for many of our clients. We offer private placement / off-market listing campaigns where your home is shown exclusively by appointment to strictly vetted, pre-qualified buyers who have signed Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).

What pre-listing preparations or staging do you recommend?

+

During our initial walkthrough, we provide a strategic punch-list of high-ROI improvements—such as lighting optimization, selective staging, paint touch-ups, and landscape refinement. We connect you with our vetted concierge vendors to ensure seamless execution.

What is the advantage of a boutique firm over a large franchise?

+

At large mega-brokerages, your property is often one of hundreds and delegated to junior coordinators. At VP Signature Group, you receive direct principal representation from Victoria Price, customized marketing budgets, agile decision-making, and undivided fiduciary devotion.
