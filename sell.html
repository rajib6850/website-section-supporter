02 // PROCESS OVERVIEW

The Selling Process

A disciplined, proven three-phase methodology designed to protect your equity and maximize transaction value.

PHASE 01

Consultation & Strategy

We conduct a comprehensive assessment of your property, analyzing micro-market trends, recent comparable sales, and target buyer demographics to establish an optimal pricing and timing strategy.

  • Comprehensive Market Analysis (CMA)
  • Asset & Architectural Evaluation
  • Strategic Pricing & Timing Blueprint
  • Pre-Listing Staging & Preparation Guidance
PHASE 02

Marketing & Exposure

Every property is presented through elevated architectural media and targeted multi-channel campaigns designed to command attention from qualified local, national, and international buyers.

  • High-Definition Architectural Photography
  • Cinematic Drone & Video Tours
  • Curated Digital & Social Campaigns
  • Private Buyer & Brokerage Network Outreach
PHASE 03

Negotiation & Results

With decades of business acumen and strategic negotiation expertise, we rigorously evaluate offers, protect your contractual interests, and navigate every detail through closing.

  • Vetting Prospective Buyer Qualifications
  • Strategic Counter-Offer Structuring
  • Inspection & Appraisal Contingency Management
  • Seamless Escrow & Closing Coordination
